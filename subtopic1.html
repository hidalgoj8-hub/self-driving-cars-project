Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)
Abstract
This Federal Highway Administration chapter describes the tech and systems that help self-driving vehicles function. The automated automobiles make use of instruments, devices, hardware, and software to detect their environment and safely navigate around it. It also provides information about how data from the sensors is used to detect different objects and plan a trip of the automated vehicle. One more important discussion is made of the various degrees of automation in driving – how much does the car do itself and how much responsibility is still put on its human operator. The importance of the source is confirmed by the existence of the technical description of the autonomous car activity.
