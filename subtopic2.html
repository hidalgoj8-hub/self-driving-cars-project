National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)
Abstract
The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration discusses the safety advantages and limitations of automated automobiles. The agency explains how automation of driving can be effective in preventing accidents by detecting hazards and responding to certain events faster than a human driver. Yet, the website makes an important clarification between driver-assistance features and fully automated cars. Almost all the cars sold in the U.S. today have the features that help with steering, stopping, and controlling the car on the lane but they do not make the car fully automated. The drivers are still responsible for driving. The value of this source lies in the fact that it gives the official information about safety and the importance of knowing automation in cars.
