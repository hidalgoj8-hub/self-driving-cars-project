Abstract
The research by Mark Ryan focuses on how self-driving cars will change transportation, but it also looks into the implications for society as a whole. In it, the author discusses the benefits which may accrue from the use of autonomous vehicles, such as having fewer traffic jams, improving productivity, reducing the number of traffic accidents, and modifying the environmental impact of transportation. There are also discussed the issues that may arise as self-driving cars become more popular. These include problems related to privacy, cybersecurity, accountability, and safety, as well as those related to the fact that the development of transportation technologies may affect people's jobs. In this respect, the research also studies how entities such as local governments, businesses, and communities should act as a result of these changes.
